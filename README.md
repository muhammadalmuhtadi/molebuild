## About

**MoleBuild** ([molebuild.com](https://molebuild.com)) is an independent, fan-made companion platform for **Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB)**. It helps players find the best hero builds, study the current meta, pick the right counters, and practice drafting — all in one place, in **English and Bahasa Indonesia**.

MoleBuild is created, owned, and operated by **Muhammad Al Muhtadi** ([@muhammadalmuhtadi](https://github.com/muhammadalmuhtadi)).

> This repository contains **information about MoleBuild only**. The application's source code is private.

## Features

### Builds
- **Build Creator** — design a full build: hero, 6 items, conditional items, emblem set, emblem talents, battle spell, and retribution type.
- **Pro Builds (MPL & M-Series)** — a curated database of tournament builds from **MPL Indonesia**, **MPL Philippines**, and **M-Series**, imported match by match.
- **Community Builds** — browse, search, like, save, and share builds made by other players.
- **Build Comparison** — compare builds side by side, including stats.
- **Patch-aware data** — every build is tied to a game patch, and items, emblems, and battle spells are shown exactly as they were in that patch.

### Heroes & Meta
- **Hero Directory** — every hero with official artwork, roles, and details.
- **Hero Tier List** — win, pick, and ban rates by rank, synced weekly from official data.
- **Top Items per Hero** — the items most used by the community for each hero and patch.
- **Items & Equipment** — full item catalogue with stats, passives, and item math (penetration, adaptive damage scaling).

### Strategy
- **Counter Pick** — a matchup database built from community votes and written reasoning.
- **Draft Simulator** — tournament (6-ban and 10-ban) and ranked 5v5 drafts, with instant counter suggestions.

### Community
- **Forum** — discussions and knowledge sharing.
- **Creator Profiles & Leaderboards** — top creators by builds, likes, and counter-pick votes.
- **News** — MLBB news gathered in one place.

## Journey

| When | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **May 2025** | **Genesis** — the first lines of code are written in an Aceh coffee shop (*warkop*) in Jakarta. The project starts as **"Legends Build"** (v0.1), tested by the squad from day one. |
| **July 2025** | **Viral wave** — introduced to streamers Prof KB, EVOS Age, Baloyskie, and Navari via Saweria; Prof KB's live reaction clip goes viral on TikTok. |
| **September 2025** | **Engine upgrade** — Draft Simulator, Counter Pick engine, and Item Math Analyzer launched. |
| **November 2025** | **From chat to stage** — attended the MPL ID Season 16 Grand Final in person and received an onstage shout-out from Prof KB. |
| **January 2026** | **Official pro meta** — curated database of MPL and M-Series pro builds added. |
| **2026** | Automated pro-build import for **MPL Indonesia and MPL Philippines**, hero data synced from official sources, and a bilingual (EN/ID) interface. |
